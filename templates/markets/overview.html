About the data

  • BTCUSD comes from public spot exchange data (Coinbase Exchange or Kraken). Volume, trades and order book belong to that one exchange.
  • XAUUSD is OTC spot gold. Prices come from a broker/aggregator feed (OANDA or Twelve Data). It has no centralized volume or order book: OANDA reports tick volume and Twelve Data reports none. No gold order-flow readings are shown.
  • If a feed is missing, stale or not configured, it is shown as unavailable and no signals are produced from it.
