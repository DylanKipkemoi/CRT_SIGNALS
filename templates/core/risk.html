Risk Disclosure

Trading leveraged or volatile instruments such as Bitcoin (BTCUSD) and spot gold (XAUUSD) carries a high level of risk and may not suit you. You can lose some or all of your capital, and with leverage you can lose more than your deposit.
  • No guaranteed outcomes. No method, including Candle Range Theory, ICT or Smart Money Concepts, wins every time. An "A+" grade means the setup met our documented rule thresholds. It does not mean the trade is likely to win.
  • Past performance. Historical statistics cover every published signal and are shown for transparency. They do not predict future results.
  • Data limitations. Spot gold trades over the counter, so there is no centralized volume or order book. Gold "volume" from brokers is tick volume, and we do not show order flow for gold. BTCUSD order-flow readings come from a single exchange's public trades and order book, which may not reflect the wider market.
  • Execution risk. Spreads, slippage, gaps, weekend closures, outages and broker differences may stop you from getting the published entry, stop or target prices.
  • Your responsibility. Use position sizing you can afford. Consider independent advice from a licensed professional. Check the regulatory status of any broker you use (for example with the Capital Markets Authority of Kenya).
