Privacy Policy

This policy explains how {{ SITE_NAME }} handles personal data, in line with the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019.

Data we collect

  • Account data: full name, email address, Kenyan phone number, hashed password (we never store or see your plain password).
  • Payment data: amount, status, M-Pesa receipt and checkout request identifiers, and a masked phone number. We never receive or store your M-Pesa PIN.
  • Usage and security data: signals you unlocked, login and payment attempts, IP address in audit logs.

How we use it

To provide the Service, enforce access rules, confirm payments with Safaricom, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal data.

Sharing

Your phone number and the payment amount are sent to Safaricom to start M-Pesa payments. Hosting and email providers process data on our behalf.

Retention and your rights

We keep payment records for as long as financial record-keeping laws require. You may ask to access, correct or delete your data through the contact page.

Security

Passwords are hashed with Django's PBKDF2 hasher, connections use HTTPS in production, and secrets are kept outside the codebase.
